Art direction, editorial, and brand identity · 1999–2020

For 21 years I served as Associate Publisher and Creative Director at Tower Publications, a Gainesville-based independent publisher. I was responsible for the visual identity, editorial design, and advertising design across three publications — Our Town (Gainesville, High Springs, and Newberry editions), Senior Times, and Gator Country — from first issue through print production.
